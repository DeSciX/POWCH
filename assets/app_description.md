# Powch

Powch is DeSciX's identity and wallet: a passkey is the identity, the wallet and vault keys derive from it in the browser, and every app reaches Powch as a cross-origin iframe at `https://powch.descix.net`. Ask this app how to integrate Powch into your own app: `DeSciX.powch` inside the App Shell, `PowchClient` from `@descix/app-sdk` on a standalone site, sign-in request shapes, consent and Site Pass, and the origin model. Answers cite the Powch developer docs they come from.
